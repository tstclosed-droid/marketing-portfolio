Case 01 · B2B Software

Webnapp — Software Development Company

Plan of action: data-driven cold outbound + local search credibility

The challenge
  • A capable dev shop with no predictable pipeline. Sales depended entirely on referrals.
  • Invisible on Google Maps and local search, so inbound was near zero.
  • No list of the local businesses that actually needed an app: restaurants, clothing stores, retail.
  • A ₹50,000 ticket size meant every wasted conversation was expensive.
What I built
  • Local SEO + Google Maps integration. Optimised the business profile, embedded Maps, and ran a structured review and ratings drive to build first-search credibility.
  • Custom Google Maps scraper. Extracted restaurant and clothing-store contact data across target areas, segmented by category and location.
  • WhatsApp API campaign engine. Category-specific pitches sent at scale, written for the pain each vertical actually feels.
  • Closed the loop. Every reply routed through a qualification flow before it reached sales.

Case 02 · FMCG & Retail

Vijaya Sweets — Premium Repositioning

Plan of action: packaging-led repositioning + on-ground community activation

The challenge
  • A quality sweet brand competing on price in a crowded market where every shop looks the same.
  • Packaging signalled "counter sweet", not "gift", so it never entered the high-value occasion basket.
  • No marketing function at all. I took the entire function over.
  • Paid advertising was inefficient for a product bought on trust and taste.
What I did
  • Rebuilt packaging first. Treated the box as the primary media asset, since a sweet box travels into every home it's gifted to.
  • Repositioned to premium. Moved the brand out of the price fight and into the gifting and celebration tier.
  • Community-led distribution. Planned and ran events inside existing local communities rather than buying reach.
  • Sampling at kitty parties and gatherings. Free tasting in the exact rooms where household purchase decisions get made, generating word-of-mouth from trusted voices.

Brand positioning shift

Moving up the value ladder instead of down the price ladder.

The three pillars
01

Packaging as media

Redesigned boxes carry the brand into every home the sweet is gifted to. Zero incremental media cost, permanent shelf presence.

02

Community activation

Events planned inside kitty parties and local gatherings, with sampling that lets the product sell itself to a captive, high-trust audience.

03

Premium price tier

Positioned above the commodity segment so margin funds quality, and quality reinforces the positioning.

Case 03 · Mobile App · Live

Sripada Astro Panchang — App Growth

Plan of action: dual engine, outbound to practitioners + organic content for consumers

The challenge
  • An astrology and panchang app that needs two very different audiences: practising astrologers and everyday users.
  • Astrologers are highly distributed across towns and districts. No ready-made database exists.
  • The core consumer audience is middle-aged and not reachable through typical performance ad targeting.
  • Paid install campaigns would be expensive relative to the app's revenue per user.
The strategy in motion
  • Reapplying the proven playbook. The Maps scraper is mining astrologer listings area by area to build a professional outreach database.
  • WhatsApp API outreach. Direct, personalised messaging positioning the app as a daily professional tool.
  • Organic astrology education video. A content engine built to capture middle-aged organic audiences who search and watch rather than click ads.
  • Two funnels, one brand. Practitioners give credibility, organic content gives volume.

The dual-engine growth model

Outbound buys speed. Organic content compounds. Running both de-risks acquisition.

Data mining WhatsApp API outreach Organic video strategy Audience segmentation + Add: installs, CPI, retention once live
